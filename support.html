Scans Log Support

Scans Log helps sonographers and sonography students log scans, track progress toward their goals, compare with classmates on an optional leaderboard, and export reports.

Contact

Questions, bugs or feature requests: email Len Burke at lennis.burke@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

Delete your account

  1. Open Scans Log and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account and type DELETE to confirm.
  3. If you signed in with Apple, confirm with Apple when asked.

This permanently removes your scans, profile, profile photo and login, and revokes Scans Log's Sign in with Apple access. It can't be undone. If you can't get into the app, email us and we'll delete it for you.

Importing scans

You can import a CSV or Excel (.xlsx) export of your scans from Settings → Import data. Imports are duplicate-safe: each row is matched by its contents, so importing the same file twice, or a newer export that overlaps an older one, won't create duplicate scans. When it finishes, the app tells you how many scans were added, how many were skipped as already imported, and which rows couldn't be read.
