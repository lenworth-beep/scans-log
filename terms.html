Scans Log Terms of Use and Community Rules

Last updated: November 28, 2026

By creating an account or using Scans Log ("the app"), you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

Using the app

Scans Log helps sonography students and professionals, including echo and vascular students, log scans and track progress. It is a personal training log, not a medical device, and must not be used for patient care decisions.

No patient information

Never enter patient names, dates of birth, record numbers, or any other information that could identify a patient, in notes, journal entries, or photos. Case photos are automatically blurred on your device before they are saved, but you are responsible for making sure no patient information is visible.

Community rules

Scans Log has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. You may not post or share content that is offensive, harassing, hateful, sexual, violent, threatening, or that includes someone else's private information, and you may not impersonate others or abuse the Team board.

Reporting and blocking

You can report or block any user from their profile. Blocking hides that person from you right away. We review reports within 24 hours.

Classes, partners and chat

You can join your classes and labs, see classmates at your school, and send pair requests. After you post a time and accept a classmate's request, you can send each other short text messages to plan your scan. Chat is text only and links are not allowed. The community rules above apply to every message. Before you send your first chat message, you agree to the partner chat rules. A chat stays until the meetup ends or is cancelled, then it is deleted once the people who still need to see the last message have read it, or 24 hours after it closes, whichever comes first. If a chat is reported, we keep it only as long as needed to review the report.

If you meet a classmate in person, meet on campus or in your lab, and report anything that feels wrong.

Enforcement

We may remove any content that breaks these rules, and we may suspend or permanently ban accounts that break them, with or without notice. If your account is suspended or banned, we do not issue refunds. All purchases and refund requests are handled by Apple under Apple's terms at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Subscriptions

Scans Log Premium is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple ID. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple ID > Subscriptions. Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA) also applies.
