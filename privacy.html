Scans Log Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Scans Log ("the app") helps sonographers and sonography students log scans, track progress and export reports. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account: when you sign in (Sign in with Apple or another supported method) we receive a user ID, and your name and email if you choose to share them.
  • Scan log: scan type, date, time, minutes and any notes you type. Notes are for your own record; do not enter patient names or other identifying patient information.
  • Profile (optional): display name, profile photo, clinical/work site, role (student or professional) and externship dates.
  • Leaderboard: unless you turn on private mode, classmates in your group can see your first name, photo, site, scan totals and average minutes. They never see your notes.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details; we only see whether your Premium subscription is active.

How we use it

To sync your log across devices, show charts and the leaderboard, send reminders you turn on, and create exports you request. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) and on your device. Widgets read a summary stored on your device.

Your choices

  • Turn on private mode to hide from the leaderboard.
  • Delete scans in the app, or reset all scans in Settings.
  • To delete your account and data, contact us at the address below.

Children

Scans Log is intended for adults in healthcare training or practice and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
