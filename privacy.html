Scans Log Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Scans Log ("the app") helps sonographers and sonography students log scans, track progress and export reports. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account: when you sign in (Sign in with Apple or another supported method) we receive a user ID, and your name and email if you choose to share them.
  • Scan log: scan type, date, time, minutes and any notes you type. Notes are for your own record; do not enter patient names or other identifying patient information.
  • Profile (optional): display name, profile photo, clinical/work site, role (student or professional) and externship dates.
  • Journal and cases (optional): journal entries, case notes, and links to your scans. These are private to your account and never shown on Team.
  • Case photos (Premium, optional): up to 2 photos per starred case. Text is automatically blurred and location/camera data removed on your device; only the blurred, resized photo is uploaded. The original never leaves your device. Do not photograph patient information.
  • School and schedule (optional): your school name, your training days, time zone and streak settings, and whether you appear only to members of your school.
  • Team board: unless you turn on private mode, classmates in your group can see your first name, photo, site, scan totals and average minutes. They never see your notes.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report or block someone, we store that so we can review reports and hide blocked users.
  • Classes and partners (optional): the classes and labs you join, partners you favorite, and pair requests. Classmates who share a class with you can see your name, photo, lab, school, the classes you are in, and this week's Partners listings. Scan totals stay private. People at your school who do not share a class cannot open that profile.
  • Partner chat: messages you send in a partner chat are stored so your partner can read them. A chat stays until the meetup ends or is cancelled, then it is deleted once the people who still need to see the last message have read it, or 24 hours after it closes. If a chat is reported, we keep a copy for up to 30 days to review the report. Blocking someone or deleting your account deletes your chats right away.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store a device token so we can tell you about pair requests, pairing updates, and partner chat messages. You can turn notifications off in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Diagnostics: anonymous crash reports and performance data (Firebase Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring) that are not linked to your identity. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs and do not track you.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details; we receive Apple's signed transaction record to confirm whether your Premium subscription is active.

How we use it

To sync your log across devices, show charts and the Team board, send reminders you turn on, and create exports you request. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Firebase Authentication, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring) and on your device. Widgets read a summary stored on your device.

Your choices

  • Turn on private mode to hide from the Team board, or choose to appear only to members of your school.
  • Delete scans in the app, or reset all scans in Settings.
  • Delete your account and all its data anytime in Settings, or contact us at the address below.
  • Report or block any user from their profile.
